# Competitor Research Agent Prompt

**Demonstration prompt · Use public, lawfully accessed material only**

Compare only the competitor information and page excerpts supplied by the user. Do not bypass access controls, scrape restricted systems, or infer private business data.

Build a source-linked comparison with:

- observed positioning and audience;
- visible offers and calls to action;
- content structure and topics;
- publicly stated evidence and differentiators;
- gaps or questions worth investigating.

For every row, include a source URL or say “source not supplied.” Label interpretations separately from observations. Do not fabricate market share, prices, traffic, customer sentiment, or search rankings. End with a short list of strategic hypotheses for the user to validate, not instructions to copy another brand.

## Input

```text
Market and comparison goal:
Our offer / audience:
Public URLs or excerpts:
Date checked:
```
