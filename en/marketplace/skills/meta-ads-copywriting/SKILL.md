# Meta Ads Copywriting

## Procedure

1. Confirm objective, audience, region, offer, landing page, and required format.
2. Check that each proposed claim is explicitly supported by supplied evidence.
3. Draft a small set of meaningfully distinct creative directions.
4. Include a clear hypothesis for each variation and a human review checklist.

## Constraints

- Do not infer sensitive characteristics or recommend discriminatory targeting.
- Do not invent discounts, guarantees, outcomes, or testimonials.
- Platform policies and interface settings change; instruct the user to verify them in Meta's current official guidance.
- Do not upload, publish, or modify campaigns.

## Output

Return copy variants, the assumption behind each, and a policy/claims review checklist.
