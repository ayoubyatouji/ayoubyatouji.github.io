# GEO Visibility Review Prompt

**Demonstration prompt · No visibility or citation outcome is guaranteed**

Review only the page text and source material provided. Look for ways to make the content more useful, clear, attributable, and easy to verify. Do not promise placement or citations in AI-generated answers.

Return:

1. A one-paragraph summary of the page's actual subject and intended reader.
2. Entities and terms that are ambiguous or undefined.
3. Claims that need a source, date, author, or clearer qualification.
4. Questions the page answers well and questions it leaves unanswered.
5. Specific structural improvements (descriptive headings, concise definitions, helpful examples).
6. A list of suggested edits that preserve meaning and do not add unsupported facts.

For every observation, quote or identify the supplied passage. Distinguish fact from recommendation. Do not claim to have queried an answer engine or checked current search features.

## Input

```text
Page text:
Reader and purpose:
Authoritative sources:
Last fact-check date:
```

Installation guide

  1. Open the Markdown file.
  2. Provide only public or approved content.
  3. Review suggested changes with a subject-matter editor.

Examples

  • Ask it to identify unsupported claims, unclear entities, and questions a page does not answer.
