# Competitor Research

## Procedure

1. Confirm the comparison objective and source date.
2. Use only public material supplied or lawfully accessed by the user.
3. Record observations with a source URL and distinguish them from interpretations.
4. Compare positioning, offers, page structure, and content coverage where evidence exists.
5. Finish with hypotheses for the user to validate.

## Constraints

- Do not bypass access controls or collect personal data.
- Do not invent market share, traffic, pricing, customer sentiment, or private strategy.
- Do not recommend copying a competitor's protected creative work.

## Output

Provide a concise, source-linked comparison and clearly labeled research gaps.

Installation guide

  1. Install in a compatible SKILL.md runtime or use manually.
  2. Provide public, relevant sources.
  3. Check observations against source pages and date them.

Examples

  • Compare user-supplied public landing pages and record the source URL for every observation.
