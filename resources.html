Quick glossary

Metrics, in plain English

ROAS
Revenue attributed to ads divided by ad spend.
CPC
Average amount paid for an ad click.
CTR
Share of impressions that result in a click.
CPA
Average cost for a defined action, such as a lead or sale.
GEO
Content optimization to improve understanding in generative engines.
UTM
Tags added to a URL to identify source, medium and campaign.
