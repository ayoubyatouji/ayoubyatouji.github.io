# GEO Content Clarity

## Procedure

1. Identify the entity, audience, question, and purpose from the supplied page.
2. Mark ambiguous terms, unsupported claims, and missing attribution.
3. Suggest useful definitions, descriptive headings, and direct answers where appropriate.
4. Keep source references attached to the claims they support.
5. Preserve nuance and subject-matter expertise.

## Constraints

- Do not promise AI citations, mentions, visibility, or rankings.
- Do not claim to have queried answer engines without an explicitly configured tool.
- Do not create sources or claims.

## Output

Return a prioritized clarity review with evidence and human fact-check steps.
