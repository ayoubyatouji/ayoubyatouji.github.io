# Google Ads Keyword Grouping

## Procedure

1. Confirm market, language, product, conversion goal, and source/date of keyword data.
2. Preserve each supplied term and group by clear intent and relevance.
3. Flag ambiguous, irrelevant, duplicate, or mixed-intent terms for human review.
4. Suggest draft ad groups and landing-page alignment.
5. List negative-keyword candidates as review questions, not automatic exclusions.

## Constraints

- Do not invent volume, CPC, competition, forecasts, or Quality Score.
- Do not upload a campaign or change account settings.
- Verify current match types and policies in official Google Ads documentation.

## Output

Provide a table of keyword, intent, suggested group, landing-page fit, and review note.
